# Ledgerline Terms of Service

_Last updated: October 2, 2026_

Ledgerline is an experimental financial research and visualization plugin created by Emit Rice.

Ledgerline provides informational tools for working with public financial data and user-supplied structured data. It is not a broker, investment adviser, accounting firm, tax adviser, or fiduciary. Outputs may contain errors, omissions, stale data, issuer-specific taxonomy differences, or imperfect filing-text matches.

Do not rely on Ledgerline as the sole basis for an investment, accounting, tax, legal, or other high-stakes financial decision. Verify material information against original sources and qualified professionals when appropriate.

Ledgerline is read-only. It does not place trades, transfer funds, or modify external financial accounts.

By using Ledgerline, you agree to use it lawfully and not to overload, scrape around, or intentionally disrupt the service or upstream public-data providers.

The service may change, be suspended, or be discontinued without notice while it remains an experimental project.
