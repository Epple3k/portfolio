# Ledgerline Support

Ledgerline is an experimental read-only financial research plugin.

For bugs, incorrect data mappings, tool-selection issues, or UI problems, open an issue in the repository:

https://github.com/Epple3k/portfolio/issues

When reporting a problem, include:
- the prompt you used,
- the ticker or FRED series involved,
- the tool name if visible,
- the expected result,
- the observed result,
- and a screenshot if the issue involves the in-chat finance canvas.

Do not include passwords, banking credentials, brokerage credentials, payment-card details, or other secrets.
