01 / Research Atlas · 2026

research atlas

A research-discovery interface for navigating large bodies of academic work without losing provenance, context, or disciplinary relationships.

role
Concept, product design, interface engineering
problem
Research discovery usually collapses complex relationships into ranked lists. I wanted a tool that lets someone move between a research question, evidence, time, and disciplinary connections without losing the thread.
what i built
An atlas around an editable research statement, provenance-aware evidence, yearly views, and a network of relationships between works and fields. The interface treats exploration as the primary interaction instead of search-result consumption.
evidence
38k+ works indexed
8k works in the active 2018–2026 ingest
5,430 interdisciplinary records
Network + timeline + provenance views
design decision
The research question stays visible and editable while every visualization changes around it, so the user's intent remains the stable object.

02 / Ledgerline · 2026

ledgerline

A finance-native ChatGPT plugin for exploring fundamentals, filings, macro data, and source-linked analysis inside the conversation.

role
Concept, product design, plugin / MCP engineering
problem
Financial research is fragmented across filings, dashboards, terminals, and search. Conversational tools make synthesis easier, but they can hide where numbers came from.
what i built
A conversational research layer that brings structured financial data into chat while preserving source links and separating retrieval from interpretation.
evidence
Source-linked company fundamentals
SEC filing context
Macro and market-data retrieval in chat
Read-only, evidence-forward interaction model
design decision
I kept the product read-only and source-forward. The point is to make the chain from question to evidence easier to inspect, not to make finance feel magical.

Node.jsMCPfinancial data APIsChatGPT

03 / Aural Field · 2026

aural field

A generative ambient sound playground for sculpting evolving tones, textures, and spatial forms through a minimal visual interface.

role
Concept, interaction design, frontend / audio prototyping
problem
Most music tools expose the machinery first. I wanted to see how little interface could remain while the experience still felt expressive.
what i built
The visual field acts as the instrument: gestures create and shape repeating audio events while controls stay deliberately secondary.
evidence
Spatial gesture-driven sound generation
Responsive browser prototype
Iterated minimal-control interaction model
design decision
The project repeatedly removes controls rather than adding them, testing whether behavior, motion, and sound can explain the system.

04 / Attention Field · 2026

attention field

An interactive visualization that turns live attention signals into a shifting field of clustered, gravitational nodes.

role
Concept, interaction design, frontend engineering
problem
Trend lists tell you what is popular, but not how attention feels as it concentrates, fragments, and moves.
what i built
A spatial system whose motion and density communicate changing public-interest relationships before the user reads a number.
design decision
I optimized for pattern recognition and curiosity rather than exact comparison, so the interface behaves more like a living map than a dashboard.
